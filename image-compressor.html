How image compression works

This tool redraws the selected image in your browser and exports it in the format and quality you choose. Lower quality often creates a smaller file, but may add visible artefacts. PNG is lossless in principle and may not shrink much through this quality setting.

Privacy note

The selected image is processed locally in your browser by this page. Do not use this tool on a shared device if you do not want other device users to access the original file.
