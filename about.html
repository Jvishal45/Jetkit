OUR PURPOSE

About JetKit

Practical tools for small digital tasks, designed to be straightforward and accessible.

What we're building

JetKit is a collection of free browser-based utilities. Our first tools help people create QR codes, compress images, and resize images without installing a separate app.

Our approach

We aim to keep each tool focused, explain what it does, and make it usable on phones as well as computers. Image processing on this version happens in your browser. The QR generator uses a third-party JavaScript library loaded from a content delivery network.

Accuracy and limitations

We test tools and improve them over time, but browser and device differences can affect results. Please verify QR codes and inspect exported images before using them for important business, print, or publishing tasks.

JetKit is an independent project. It is not affiliated with Google or any social media platform.
