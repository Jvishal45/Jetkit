ABOUT OUR PROJECT

About JetKit

Simple, free, and practical online tools designed to make everyday digital tasks easier.

What is JetKit?

JetKit is a collection of free online utilities created to help people complete common digital tasks quickly and easily. Our goal is to provide useful tools through a simple website that works on both mobile phones and desktop computers.

You can use our tools directly in your browser without installing a separate application or creating an account.

Our Online Tools

1. QR Code Generator

Create QR codes from text, website links, and other supported information. QR codes make it easier to share information by allowing people to scan them using a compatible smartphone camera or QR scanner.

Try our QR Code Generator →

2. Image Compressor

Reduce image file sizes to help make images easier to store, share, and upload. Image compression can be useful when you need smaller files for websites, documents, or online forms.

Try our Image Compressor →

3. Image Resizer

Resize images to dimensions that better suit your needs. This can help when preparing images for websites, social media, documents, or other digital projects.

Try our Image Resizer →

Our Approach

We believe online tools should be easy to understand and convenient to use. We aim to keep our tools focused, provide clear instructions, and make the website accessible across different screen sizes.

Image compression and resizing on the current version are performed in your browser. The QR Code Generator uses a third-party JavaScript library loaded from a content delivery network.

Quality and Limitations

We aim to improve JetKit by reviewing our tools and making the experience more useful. However, results may vary depending on your browser, device, image format, and input.

Please review downloaded images and test generated QR codes before using them for important business, printing, or publishing purposes.
