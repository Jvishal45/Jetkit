Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This is a starter privacy policy for the JetKit project. Review and adapt it to match the services, hosting, analytics, and advertising that are actually enabled before publishing.

Files and browser tools

The image compressor and image resizer process selected image files in your browser using browser APIs. This static version does not intentionally upload those selected images to a JetKit server. The QR generator sends the text you enter to a JavaScript QR library running in your browser; the library is currently loaded from a third-party CDN, so the browser makes a request to that provider to download the script.

Hosting and technical data

If hosted on GitHub Pages or another hosting provider, that provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request logs according to its own policies. Read the hosting provider's current privacy documentation.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This starter site does not include analytics tags or AdSense code. If you later add analytics, advertising, affiliate links, or other third-party services, update this policy to describe the services, cookies or similar technologies, data use, and any choices available to visitors. Ad services may use cookies or other identifiers according to their own settings and policies.

Children and sensitive information

Do not enter confidential, sensitive, or personal information into QR codes. A QR code can be scanned by anyone who obtains it. Do not use these tools as a secure document-storage or secret-sharing service.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method listed on the Contact page. Add a real monitored contact address before public launch.

Important: This template is general information, not legal advice. Confirm the policy fits your actual website and applicable laws.
