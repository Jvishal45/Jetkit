Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Welcome to JetKit. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit our website and use our free online tools.

1. Information We Process

JetKit does not require visitors to create an account to use its basic online tools.

When you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your enquiry.

2. Image Tools and File Processing

Our Image Compressor and Image Resizer are designed to process selected image files directly in your browser using browser APIs.

The current versions of these tools do not intentionally upload selected image files to a JetKit server. Processing takes place on your device.

You should still avoid using the tools with confidential or highly sensitive images.

3. QR Code Generator

Our QR Code Generator processes the text entered by visitors in their browser using a JavaScript QR code library.

The library is loaded from a third-party content delivery network (CDN). Your browser may contact that provider to download the library. Third-party providers may process technical request information under their own privacy policies.

Do not enter passwords, confidential information, financial details, or private personal information into QR codes.

4. Website Hosting and Technical Information

JetKit is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, device information, and server request logs for security, reliability, and service operation.

Please review GitHub's Privacy Statement for more information about its data practices.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

JetKit's current website version does not intentionally use analytics or advertising tracking scripts. However, third-party services used by the website, including hosting providers and external script providers, may process technical information.

If cookies, analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to explain their use and any available visitor choices.

6. Google AdSense and Advertising

At the time of this policy update, JetKit has not activated Google AdSense advertising.

If Google AdSense is introduced after approval, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize advertisements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a visitor's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Visitors can learn more about Google's advertising practices and manage personalized advertising preferences through:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • AboutAds advertising choices

When advertising is activated, this policy will be updated with the applicable advertising providers and additional details about their data practices.

7. Third-Party Services and External Links

JetKit may contain links to external websites and services. We do not control the privacy practices, content, or security of those third-party websites.

Visitors should review the privacy policies of external services before using them.

8. Children's Privacy

JetKit's tools are general-purpose online utilities. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children through account registration. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email, please contact us.

9. Data Security

We aim to keep JetKit simple and minimize unnecessary collection of personal information. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Please do not submit passwords, payment information, confidential documents, or other sensitive data through our tools or email.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, tools, hosting arrangements, or advertising services change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how JetKit handles information, contact us:

Email: politecuts777@gmail.com

This policy provides general information about JetKit's practices and is not legal advice.
